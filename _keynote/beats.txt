# Optional hand-written "idea beats" for Ideas grading mode.
#
# By default every card is graded by its sentences: a sentence counts as
# covered when at least half of its key words are heard, in any order.
# To grade a card by your own list of ideas instead, add a block here:
#
#   card 5
#   Students are turning in their blue books :: blue books ; turning in / handing in / finishing
#   Micah hands me his book and gives me a bear hug :: hug ; hands it to me / hands me ; off the ground / lifts
#
# One line per idea: a plain-English label, then "::", then the key phrases
# separated by ";". Alternatives for the same key are separated by "/".
# An idea counts as covered when at least half of its keys are heard.
# Cards not listed here keep the automatic sentence-based beats.
# Run `node _keynote/build.js` after editing.
